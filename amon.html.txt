LESSON ATOM , ELEMENT AND COMPOUND 
Atoms are smallest particles of an element that that take part in a chemical reaction.
STRUCTURE OF AN ATOM 
1 Neutrons 
2 protons
3 electrons 
Neutrons: has no charge and are located on the centre of a nucleus(0).
protons: are positively charged particles that are located on the centre of a nucleus(+).
electrons: are negatively charged particles that are located on the shell or energy levels(-).
ELEMENT 
An element is a pure substance.
In other ways an element is a substance made of one type a material that cannot be split by any chemical or physical means.
NOTE:that a pure substance is a substance made of one type of material.e.g oxygen (o2),nitrogen(N2) ect.
COMPOUND 
A compound is a combination of two or more atoms that are chemically combined or joined together e.g amonia(NH3)
